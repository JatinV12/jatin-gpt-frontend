Model Limitations & Research Scope

This model is an educational research project built and trained from scratch to demonstrate full-stack transformer pretraining and lexical RAG. Because it is a compact model rather than a commercial multi-billion parameter LLM, please keep the following architectural limitations in mind:

1. Parameter Scale (51 Million Params) Modern LLMs have 8 to 70 billion parameters. With 51M parameters, the model has limited internal memory capacity. Without context guidance, answers may exhibit grammatical variance or slight hallucination.
2. TinyStories Pretraining Prior The base model was pretrained on 474M tokens of synthetic children's stories. When running in "Pretrained Base" mode without RAG, the model naturally completes sentences in simple narrative prose rather than technical discourse.
3. Why RAG Mode Is Recommended RAG injects verified factual domain excerpts directly into the prompt. This anchors the 51M attention heads on real facts, compensating for the small model capacity and ensuring factual grounding.
4. BPE Sub-Word Boundaries GPT-2 Byte-Pair Encoding splits rare proper nouns and technical terms into multiple subword tokens. Decoding applies a repetition penalty (1.25) and constrained greedy sampling to prevent stuttering.
